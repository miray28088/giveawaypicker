💡 How to get your Instagram Session Cookie in 15 seconds:

  1. Open Instagram.com in Google Chrome or Edge and log into your account.
  2. Press F12 (or right-click anywhere and click Inspect) to open Developer Tools.
  3. Click the Application tab at the top (or Storage in Firefox).
  4. In the left sidebar, expand Cookies and select https://www.instagram.com.
  5. Find the row named sessionid, double-click its Value, copy it, and paste it into Method 2 above!
